Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Aiko yutu ("the app") is a private tool used solely by the owner of the Aiko YouTube channel to manage that channel. It uses YouTube API Services.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the app accesses the owner's YouTube account data through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. This includes channel details, videos and their metadata, playlists, comments, captions and channel analytics reports.

How the data is used

The data is used only to display, analyze and update the owner's own YouTube channel at the owner's request. The app does not use the data for advertising and does not sell it.

Storage and sharing

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Google access tokens are stored only in local files on that computer. The app has no server, and YouTube data is not collected, stored or shared with third parties by the app's developer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files also removes the app's access.

Google and YouTube policies

Use of the app is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Aiko YouTube channel.
